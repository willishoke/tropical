## 1. Shipping decision

Ship the accepted mono `current_radii` grouped transfer on the four authored
Metal hits, with a live bipolar `POSITION` control:

```text
POSITION = -1   classic reverse-reverb pre-tail
POSITION =  0   equal-power two-sided bloom
POSITION = +1   accepted causal room
```

`POSITION` replaces `LENGTH`. `ROOM` remains the glided wet-return level.
`FLOW` remains the independent whole-scene address/velocity control. The dry
Metal hit is always causal relative to its scene anchor; POSITION moves only
the room field.

The production contract is mono. Stereo was useful evidence that the grouped
representation could carry a diffuse side field, but the accepted MVP audition
was mono and the shipping engine has one output sink. Do not widen the engine
or asset ABI for stereo in this release.

The grouped room is applied only to `metalHit1` through `metalHit4`. Remove the
old chord-room sends from the wet bus rather than retaining a rejected sparse
room beside the selected one. The filtered string lane and direct Metal impact
lane otherwise remain unchanged.

Mutable Instruments Clouds at official Eurorack commit
`08460a69a7e1f7a81c5a2abcc7189c9a6b7208d4` remains the frozen audition oracle.
No Clouds or Freeverb code is linked, run, or required by the product.

## 2. Frozen scope

The production feature is deliberately narrow:

- one fixed room profile;
- one native engine sample rate, 44,100 Hz;
- twelve periodic pole-lattice groups;
- twelve fixed Metal source pole coordinates reused by all four hits;
- forward and classic anti-causal wet responses;
- one 20 ms glided POSITION parameter;
- direct analytic evaluation first, with one fixed-scene cache fallback; and
- qualification on the recorded release Mac at `Bdev=128`, `Rgpu=512`.

There is no live room size, RT60, geometry, modulation, profile selection, or
arbitrary-input reverse reverb in this release. A longer audition WAV is not a
feature: the selected stable transfer already has infinite mathematical
support. Changing decay length means fitting and approving a different asset
and is deferred.

## 3. Gate 0: regenerate at the shipping sample rate

The accepted audition cannot be copied into production unchanged. Its grouped
fit and exact proofs were evaluated at Clouds' 32 kHz native rate; the audition
WAV was then resampled to 44.1 kHz. Tropical renders natively at 44.1 kHz.
Running a hidden 32 kHz room subgraph would add a sample-rate architecture that
the release has not qualified.

Before compiler integration:

1. parameterize the grouped-fit and reverse-position reproducers by sample
   rate;
2. resample the frozen Clouds target to 44.1 kHz once, outside the optimizer;
3. fit the same `current_radii` architecture at native 44.1 kHz using periods
   `1116, 1188, 1277, 1356, 1422, 1491, 1557, 1617, 1112, 882, 682, 450`;
4. render wet impulse, forward Metal hit, reverse Metal hit, and a POSITION
   scrub directly at 44.1 kHz;
5. repeat the forward-convolution and literal reverse-operation proofs; and
6. obtain a listening pass confirming that the native-rate fit preserves the
   accepted scale and density.

This is a productionization gate, not an architecture referendum. Fitting
weights and conditioning may be adjusted to recover the accepted sound while
period count, group count, mono output, control semantics, and cost envelope
remain frozen. If the native-rate candidate cannot pass listening within that
envelope, stop and record the room lane as blocked.

The native periods contain 14,150 carrier samples. For twelve source pole
coordinates, one complex64 prefix direction is 1,358,400 bytes; forward and
reverse together are 2,716,800 bytes (2.59 MiB), before a small header and
metadata. These replace the obsolete 32 kHz payload figures in earlier notes.

## 4. DSP contract

### 4.1 Accepted grouped transfer

For a source row and one room group, use

```text
x(u) = C exp(lambda u / Fs) gate(u)
h[k] = r^k c[k mod P] gate(k),  k integer
```

where `u` is the signed scene coordinate in samples relative to the hit anchor,
`lambda = -sigma + i omega`, `C` is the authored complex residue, `P` is the
group period, `r` is its stable radius, and `c` is the frozen periodic carrier.
The output is the real part of the sum over twelve source rows and twelve room
groups.

This is a discrete-time room acting on Tropical's continuously addressable
analytic source. It is exact at integer coordinates and has a direct extension
to fractional coordinates, which is required by `FLOW`.

### 4.2 Causal arm

For `u >= 0`, define

```text
m = floor(u)
q = m div P
R = m mod P
p = exp(lambda / Fs)
rho = r / p
G = rho^P
A[R] = sum_(s=0..R) c[s] rho^s
S(L, u) = p^u * sum_(j=0..L-1) G^j
```

Then

```text
y_forward(u) = C * (A[R] * S(q + 1, u)
  + (A[P-1] - A[R]) * S(q, u))
```

and `y_forward(u) = 0` for `u < 0`. `A` is precomputed for every unique
source/group pair. Never form `G^q` on its own: source poles can decay much
faster than the room, making that intermediate overflow even though the answer
is finite. Use the already-proven scaled form

```text
S(L, u) = (p^u - r^(P L) p^(u - P L)) / (1 - G)
```

and its `L p^u` limit near `G=1`, with the same threshold and operation order
in the Python, JIT, and Metal implementations.

### 4.3 Classic reverse arm

For any real `u`, define

```text
d = max(ceil(-u), 0)
p = exp(lambda / Fs)
z = p r
B[R] = sum_(s=0..P-1) c[(R+s) mod P] z^s
```

Then

```text
y_reverse(u) = C exp(lambda (u + d) / Fs)
  * r^d * B[d mod P] / (1 - z^P)
```

`B` is also precomputed for every unique source/group pair. At integer `u`
this is the already-proven formula. At fractional `u`, `d` selects the first
causal future source sample while the analytic envelope retains the remaining
fractional age. Gate 0 must add a literal fractional-coordinate oracle rather
than relying only on this derivation.

A handoff-time numerical check used the existing asset, one authored source
row, all twelve groups, and representative positive and negative fractional
coordinates. The scaled causal form agreed with direct summation to
`1.43e-13` worst relative error; the fractional reverse form agreed to
`2.50e-14`. Production still requires the full twelve-row native-rate oracle.

This arm is exactly the fixed-scene operation
`reverse(source) -> causal room -> reverse(result)`. It is not the incumbent
`modalReverb` direction path, which mirrors the already-composed source and
room and measured `1.0` NRMSE against the requested operation. No production
code may route POSITION through `modalBankSigDir` or reinterpret its `dir`
parameter.

### 4.4 POSITION mix

Clamp POSITION to `[-1, +1]` and use

```text
g_reverse = sqrt((1 - POSITION) / 2)
g_forward = sqrt((1 + POSITION) / 2)
wet = g_reverse * reverse + g_forward * forward
```

The control is a normal stateless 20 ms `glideknob`, anchored to output time so
its gesture does not run backward when FLOW is negative. Both wet arms remain
present for the measured direct-path cost. Endpoint specialization is optional
only after the unspecialized path passes and may not change sound or control
latency.

The midpoint is a two-sided equal-power bloom, not a rigid translation of one
tail. That is the intended product behavior.

## 6. Production asset contract

Generate, do not hand-edit, these checked artifacts:

```text
playground/assets/grouped-room/clouds-current-radii-mono-v1-44100.tgrm
playground/assets/grouped-room/clouds-current-radii-mono-v1-44100.json
```

The JSON manifest is reviewable provenance. It records:

- profile and binary-format versions;
- `sample_rate=44100`, `group_count=12`, `source_pole_count=12`;
- ordered periods and radii;
- ordered source `(frequency, sigma)` coordinates;
- table order, offsets, counts, scalar format, and endianness;
- generator revision and command;
- hashes of the frozen Clouds target and fitted-carrier source;
- SHA-256 of the binary payload; and
- the oracle measurements and audition filenames that approved it.

The binary is little-endian with a versioned fixed header followed by aligned
metadata, source-major/group-major forward complex64 tables, and
source-major/group-major reverse complex64 tables. Amplitude, phase, and hit
anchor are not stored: all four hits share the same twelve pole coordinates and
apply their authored residues and anchors at runtime.

Treat the generator plus manifest as the source of truth. Audition `.npz` files
are evidence inputs, not runtime assets. A checked generator run must reproduce
the payload hash.

### Immutable runtime binding

Add an optional immutable-asset array class to the plan/runtime instead of
serializing hundreds of thousands of JSON numbers or rebuilding prefixes in an
audio callback:

- plans without assets remain Plan-5 and byte-compatible;
- a plan containing immutable assets emits Plan-6 and names a package-relative
  path, byte count, SHA-256, sample rate, and array-slot offset table;
- the parser accepts Plan-5 and Plan-6, rejects absolute paths and `..`, and
  validates every bound and count;
- asset I/O, hashing, conversion, and allocation complete before kernel
  publication and before audio starts;
- JIT array pointers reference immutable host-owned storage;
- Metal receives one packed read-only asset buffer at `buffer(4)`, uploaded
  once when the kernel is created, not copied into every render request;
- existing generation-buffered coefficient columns remain at `buffer(3)` and
  are not inflated with static room tables; and
- kernel/hot-swap ownership keeps the asset alive until no render can reference
  it.

No asset path lookup, disk I/O, allocation, hash, large copy, lock, or GPU
submission may occur on the audio callback. A missing, mismatched, truncated,
wrong-rate, or non-finite asset fails load loudly; it never produces a silent
room as a fallback.

## 7. Compiler and backend implementation

Reuse the existing signed relative-clock path, negative-time gates, banked
reductions, stateless sample-index glide, seek/render-window addressing, and
JIT/Metal emitters. Do not build a second transport or parameter scheduler.
The new engine work is limited to the grouped terminal node, its exact
reduction, and immutable table transport.

### Decoder and modal seam

1. Add `groupedroom` and its `position` port to the vocabulary.
2. Decode only the frozen profile name and emit the dedicated Patch node.
3. Teach graph ranking/type checks that this node is a modal consumer and
   signal producer.
4. Lower its input without first composing an ordinary room or realizing the
   incumbent direction path.
5. Assert exactly the expected twelve source pole coordinates for this profile;
   a changed Metal instrument must regenerate the asset rather than reading the
   wrong tables.

### Flat IR

Emit one nested twelve-source by twelve-group reduction. Share per-source
coordinate work and per-pair constants across the forward and reverse arms.
Each pair performs bounded arithmetic and one indexed complex read per arm;
there is no loop over the group period at runtime.

The relative coordinate is signed. Use `floor(u)` for the causal residue and
`ceil(-u)` for the reverse residue before converting to bounded integer
quotient/remainder. Preserve real `u` for the analytic exponential. Explicitly
gate the causal arm for negative `u`; the reverse arm remains defined on both
sides. Sum in the frozen source/group order so JIT/Metal comparisons are
reproducible.

### JIT and Metal

- Add immutable-array indexed reads to LLVM and MSL emission.
- Bind the packed asset at a distinct ABI index and freeze that ABI in golden
  tests.
- Keep Metal safe math; do not enable reassociation, contraction, or fast math
  to win the probe.
- Convert/load complex64 deterministically and use the same table order on both
  backends.
- Keep all parameter state in the existing POSITION glide slots; the room
  profile never participates in coefficient epochs.
- Add fixed-complexity assertions for 12 sources, 12 groups, 144 pairs per hit,
  and four hits.

## 8. Cost decision and fallback

### Preferred direct evaluator

At an intermediate POSITION:

| Item | Production cost |
|---|---:|
| Source/group pairs per hit, both arms | 288 equivalents |
| Four fixed hits, both arms | 1,152 equivalents |
| Native mono forward + reverse prefix payload | 2.59 MiB |

This is the first implementation because it preserves the strongest claim:
the wet field is analytically addressable at arbitrary scene coordinates and
the control is not crossfading two rendered recordings.

### Bounded fixed-scene fallback

If the direct evaluator fails release-Mac reserve after obvious sharing and
indexing fixes, freeze two 16-second mono float32 wet bases: one causal and one
classic reverse. They require 5,644,800 bytes (5.38 MiB) total. Address them
from the same master scene coordinate and apply the identical equal-power
POSITION mix.

Because FLOW can produce fractional coordinates, the cached path must use a
documented interpolating read and pass a FLOW sweep against the analytic
reference. It remains valid only for this exact source, four anchors, room,
sample rate, and scene duration. Any graph change regenerates both bases.

Select the fallback only when the direct path fails a recorded performance
gate and the cached path passes endpoint, POSITION, FLOW, seek, listening,
headroom, and zero-fault gates. Record the selected path in the decision log
and qualification manifest. Do not weaken runtime gates, reduce the effect to
the incumbent mirror, or add stateful callback DSP.

## 10. Acceptance matrix

### Asset and reference

- native 44.1 kHz wet impulse preserves the accepted scale and density by
  listening;
- forward grouped output vs ordinary convolution: relative error `< 1e-9` in
  the complex128 oracle;
- reverse grouped output vs literal reverse/source/room/reverse: relative error
  `< 1e-8` at integer and fractional coordinates;
- all periods, radii, carrier values, prefix values, and outputs are finite;
- manifest and runtime payload hashes reproduce; and
- a wrong hash, size, rate, profile, or source coordinate refuses to load.

### Semantics

- `POSITION=+1` matches the accepted causal arm within backend tolerance;
- `POSITION=-1` matches the classic literal operation, not whole-output
  mirroring;
- `POSITION=0` uses the specified equal-power gains;
- dry Metal remains causal and unchanged at all POSITION values;
- five fixed POSITION values move pre-event energy and centroid monotonically;
- a seek to scene time four seconds at `POSITION=-1` returns finite, nonzero
  wet audio before the six-second dry hit;
- forward, stopped, fractional-speed, and reverse FLOW coordinates agree with
  the oracle; and
- a live scrub is click-free and retains the first/reversal/final gesture
  contract.

### Backend and performance

- JIT vs the asset-quantized oracle: relative error `<= 1e-9`;
- Metal vs JIT: NRMSE `<= 1e-5` and max absolute error `<= 1e-4`;
- no runtime period loop or accidental source-mode by room-pole expansion;
- immutable room data is uploaded once per kernel load, not per tile or epoch;
- cold compile is `<= 10 s` and warm load is `<= 3 s` on the release Mac;
- scheduled-write and audible-activation latency meet the master thresholds;
- every runtime, Metal, callback, DAC, non-finite, and silent-block fault count
  is zero through smoke and soak; and
- the final scene stays below the frozen headroom limit without a limiter.

### Listening

- the forward endpoint remains the accepted large-room MVP;
- the reverse endpoint is unmistakable before the second Metal hit;
- the POSITION continuum reads as one intentional temporal gesture;
- strings and dry impacts remain clear when ROOM and POSITION move; and
- final headphones/monitor capture is approved.

If the reverse endpoint is too subtle, the only admissible sound-design fork is
a separately auditioned longer fitted decay or transient-only room send. Do not
substitute the incorrect whole-composite mirror. This is the only remaining
decision that can reopen sound design.

## 11. Demo choreography

Metal hits occur at 2, 6, 10, and 14 seconds. The zero boundary clips part of
the first pre-tail, so the selling moment uses the second hit:

1. start at `POSITION=+1` and let the two-second hit establish the room;
2. from roughly 3.0 to 4.5 seconds, scrub toward `-1` while FLOW remains
   forward;
3. hold long enough for the room to gather before the dry hit at six seconds;
4. cross through zero toward `+1` before a later hit to show the continuum; and
5. optionally move FLOW only after POSITION has been understood as independent
   room-time placement.

A live control cannot change samples already emitted by the DAC. It reveals the
remaining analytically known pre-tail immediately and fully affects later
scheduled hits. Unscheduled live input is outside the release claim.
